🛑 The Problem

Global freight markets are highly volatile, making it challenging to identify optimal entry points for charter contracts.

  • Reactive Decision-Making: Relying on daily market exploration leads to missed cost savings.
  • Vessel Inefficiencies: Hard to determine the most suitable vessel type accounting for port limits.
  • Lack of Future Visibility: No robust forecasting mechanism for rate trends.

🎯 Our Objective

Develop a predictive, AI-driven dashboard that optimizes maritime logistics procurement.

  • Data-Driven Forecasts: 90-day predictive modeling for freight rates.
  • Proactive Strategy: Shift from reactive spot-buying to strategic contract procurement.
  • Risk Mitigation: Identify seasonal weather risks and port congestion before they impact operations.

✨ Key Features

A comprehensive suite for the modern chartering desk:

  1. Market Rate Forecast (Holt-Winters synthetic modeling)
  2. Vessel Optimizer (Cost-per-MT calculation based on port draft limits)
  3. Route Analysis (Transit times & bunker consumption)
  4. Contract Strategy Advisor (Spot vs. COA ROI comparison)
  5. Live Telematics (Real-time global fleet tracking)
  6. Freight AI Assistant (Conversational predictive insights)

📈 Intelligent Rate Forecasting

Predicting the unpredictable.

  • 90-Day Outlook: Projects rates for Capesize, Panamax, Supramax, and Handysize.
  • Macro-Economic Indicators: Integrates BDI (Baltic Dry Index) and Coal price volatility.
  • Actionable Data: Allows procurement teams to lock in rates when the predictive curve indicates an upcoming surge.

🚢 Route Analysis & Optimizer

The right vessel, on the right route, at the right time.

  • Draft & Dimension Limits: Cross-references origin and destination port constraints.
  • Normalized Cost Calculation: Computes the true Cost per Metric Ton ($/MT).
  • Optimal Routing: Compares transit times across Australian, South African, and Indonesian corridors.

💼 Contract Strategy Advisor

Optimizing procurement capital.

  • Dynamic Recommendations: Should we buy on the Spot market, or secure a 6-month Contract of Affreightment (COA)?
  • ROI Calculation: Analyzes the delta between current spot rates and the 90-day forecast.
  • Risk Scoring: Evaluates the volatility risk of committing to long-term contracts.

📡 Live Telematics & 🧠 AI

Next-generation operational awareness.

  • Global Fleet Telematics: Real-time visualization of active voyages using interactive mapping. Tracks delays and transit progress.
  • AI Assistant: A conversational engine that monitors idle times, seasonal weather risks (e.g., cyclones), and provides on-demand strategic advice.
